"How old are you?" usually has a one-number answer, but forms, eligibility rules and personal curiosity sometimes need more: years, months and even days. Here is how that is worked out, and what trips people up.

## The method

The [Age Calculator](/tools/age-calculator/) uses a calendar approach:

1. Count **whole months** from the date of birth to the chosen date.
2. Years are those whole months divided by 12. The remainder is the months.
3. The **days** are what is left after the last complete month.

Born 15 May 1990, on 20 August 2024: that is 34 years, 3 months and 5 days.

## Why month lengths make it tricky

Months have different lengths, so "one month later" is not always 30 days. What is one month after 31 January? There is no 31 February. The calculator moves to the **last day of the shorter month** (28 or 29 February), so 31 January to 1 March counts as 1 month and 1 day.

## Leap years and 29 February

People born on 29 February have a birthday only in leap years. In other years it is shown as 28 February in our calculator. Legal systems differ: some treat 1 March as the date a person turns a year older. If the exact date matters for a legal purpose, check the rule that applies to you.

## Age on a past or future date

The "Age on" date lets you answer practical questions:

- Will someone be old enough on the closing date of an application?
- How old was someone on a particular day?
- How old will a child be at the start of the school year?

## Total days, weeks and months

Besides years, months and days, the tool shows total months, weeks and days lived. For two dates in the same year, total days is simply the difference in the day-of-year numbers. Over many years it must account for leap days, which the calculator does for you.

## Next birthday

The calculator also gives the date of your next birthday, the weekday it falls on, and the number of days to wait. If your birthday is today, it says so.

## Checking by hand

1. Subtract the birth year from the current year.
2. If this year's birthday has not happened yet, subtract one.
3. For months and days, count forward from the last birthday.

## FAQ

### Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent.

### Why is my age in days slightly different on another website?

Some tools count the start date as day 1, or use hours and time zones. This calculator counts calendar days between the two dates.

### Does it work for dates before 1900?

The date picker accepts dates from 1900. Older dates are outside what the tool supports.
