*This article is general education, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional about your own health.*

Body mass index (BMI) is a number that relates weight to height. It is quick and free to calculate, which is why it is widely used for screening populations. It is much less useful as a verdict on one individual.

## How BMI is calculated

**BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²**

A person who is 1.75 m tall and weighs 70 kg: 1.75 × 1.75 = 3.0625, and 70 ÷ 3.0625 = **22.9**.

With pounds and inches, the formula is weight × 703 ÷ height². The [BMI Calculator](/tools/bmi-calculator/) accepts either system and does the conversion for you.

## The adult categories

The World Health Organization groups adult BMI as follows:

| BMI | Category |
| --- | --- |
| Below 18.5 | Underweight |
| 18.5 to 24.9 | Normal weight |
| 25 to 29.9 | Overweight |
| 30 to 34.9 | Obesity, class I |
| 35 to 39.9 | Obesity, class II |
| 40 and above | Obesity, class III |

These categories apply to adults aged 18 and over. Children and teenagers are assessed using age- and sex-specific growth charts, not these cut-offs.

## What BMI cannot tell you

- **Muscle versus fat.** A muscular person can have a high BMI and low body fat.
- **Where fat is stored.** Fat around the abdomen carries different health implications than fat elsewhere.
- **Differences between groups.** Health-risk thresholds may differ for people of different ethnic backgrounds, and for older adults.
- **Overall health.** Blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, fitness, diet and family history are not in the equation.

Clinicians therefore use BMI as one data point alongside others, such as waist circumference and blood tests.

## Using the result sensibly

1. Treat it as a starting point for a conversation, not a diagnosis.
2. Use the healthy-weight range shown by the calculator as a reference for your height only.
3. If your result is outside the normal range, or you are worried about your weight, speak with a healthcare professional.
4. Do not use BMI to make decisions about a child's weight; ask a paediatrician.

## A note on change over time

Weight naturally changes during life, and one measurement is a snapshot. If you track your own changes, note the trend rather than a single number, and consider percentage change with the [Percentage Calculator](/tools/percentage-calculator/).

## FAQ

### Is BMI accurate during pregnancy?

No. BMI is not designed for pregnancy. Follow the advice of your midwife or doctor.

### Is a BMI of 25 "unhealthy"?

It is the boundary of a statistical category, not a switch between healthy and unhealthy. Many other factors matter.

No. The calculation runs in your browser.
