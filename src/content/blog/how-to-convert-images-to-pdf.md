A PDF is easier to send, print and archive than a folder of loose pictures. Receipts, signed forms, ID photos, screenshots and photographed pages all work better as a single document. Here is how to get a clean result.

## Step by step

1. Open the [Image to PDF converter](/tools/image-to-pdf/) and add your JPG, PNG or WebP files.
2. Use the arrows to put the images in the right order. The first image becomes page 1.
3. Choose a **page size**, **orientation** and **margin**.
4. Create the PDF and download it.

Everything happens in your browser, so private photos are not uploaded.

## Choosing a page size

| Option | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **A4** (210 × 297 mm) | Documents in most of the world |
| **US Letter** (8.5 × 11 in) | Documents in the United States and Canada |
| **Fit to image** | Receipts, screenshots, and anything that should not get white borders |

Pick the size your recipient prints on. A PDF made for A4 will be scaled slightly when printed on Letter paper and the other way round.

## Orientation

"Automatic" makes a landscape page for a wide picture and a portrait page for a tall one. This avoids squeezing a landscape photo onto a portrait page. Choose portrait or landscape to force every page the same.

## Order matters

Name or sort your files before you add them (01, 02, 03). The converter keeps your order, and you can still move pages up or down before you create the PDF.

## Keeping the file small

Phone photos are large. Twenty pictures at 4 MB each will make an 80 MB PDF that no email accepts. Two ways to shrink it:

- **Before converting:** shrink the pictures with the [Image Compressor](/tools/image-compressor/). A longest side of about 2000 pixels is usually plenty for reading on screen.
- **After converting:** run the PDF through the [PDF Compressor](/tools/compress-pdf/) in Balanced mode. Because the pages are already pictures, nothing selectable is lost.

## Photos that look sideways

Phones record rotation in a hidden tag. The converter reads that tag, so pictures should appear upright. If one looks wrong, rotate it in your gallery app and add it again.

## Combining with other documents

To join the new PDF with existing PDFs, use the [PDF Merger](/tools/merge-pdf/). For a full workflow with phone scans, read [how to scan documents with your phone](/blog/scan-documents-with-phone-into-pdf/).

## FAQ

### Do JPG and PNG images keep their quality?

JPEGs are re-encoded at 92% quality, which is visually very close to the original. PNG and WebP images are embedded losslessly.

### Can I mix JPG, PNG and WebP in one PDF?

Yes. All three can be combined.

### Is the text in a photographed page searchable?

No. The PDF contains pictures, not text. You would need OCR software to make it searchable.
